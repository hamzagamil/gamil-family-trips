Delete your Gamil Family Trips account

In the app (iOS, Android or web)

  1. Open Gamil Family Trips, or go to daily-trip-planner.web.app, and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture (Profile).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

By email

If you can't sign in, email hamzagamil@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, photo, account type) and sign-in account
  • Your frequent trips, notifications and push-notification device tokens
  • Your place on every trip you joined
  • Trips you created (removed for everyone)

Deletion is permanent and happens immediately.
