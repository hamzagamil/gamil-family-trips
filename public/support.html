Gamil Family Trips Support

Gamil Family Trips helps a family coordinate shared daily trips: create a trip, join as a rider or driver, and get reminded before departure.

Contact

Email hamzagamil@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Use Sign in with Google, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone.
  • I'm not getting notifications. Open your profile and tap Push Notifications. If they are blocked, turn them on in your phone's Settings for Gamil Family Trips. New-trip alerts go to drivers; departure reminders go to everyone on the trip.
  • How do I change between Driver and Rider? Open your profile and pick your account type.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
