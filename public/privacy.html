Gamil Family Trips Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Gamil Family Trips ("we", "the app") helps a team coordinate shared daily trips. This policy explains what data the Gamil Family Trips app (iOS, Android and web at daily-trip-planner.web.app) collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Data we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email address, profile photo (if any) and a unique user ID. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's relay address.
  • Account type: whether you chose Driver or Rider.
  • Trip data: trips you create (destination, date and time), trips you join, and the frequent-trip templates you save.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token for each device where you turn on notifications, and the in-app notification messages sent to you (for example "Alex joined your trip").

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, payment information or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show trips and who is on them to the other members of Gamil Family Trips.
  • To send push notifications about new trips, driver changes and departure reminders.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see your data

  • Other Gamil Family Trips users can see your name and profile photo on trips you create or join, and the trips themselves.
  • Service providers: Gamil Family Trips runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and Hosting), which stores and processes the data above on our behalf. See the Firebase privacy information. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If you have no profile photo, your initials are sent to ui-avatars.com to draw a placeholder avatar.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, frequent trips, notifications and device tokens, removes you from all trips, removes the trips you created, and deletes your sign-in account. See how to delete your account for other options.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by Firebase security rules, so only signed-in users can read trips and only you can read your own profile and notifications.

Children

Gamil Family Trips is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for significant changes notify you in the app.
